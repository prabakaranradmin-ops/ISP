Bandwidth profiles

A profile is the shaping a plan delivers: the ceiling, an optional committed floor, an optional burst, and the queue priority. Plans share profiles, so an operator runs a handful of these against dozens of tariffs that differ only in price, quota and validity. Editing a profile re-shapes every plan on it — each subscriber from their next authentication, not immediately.
