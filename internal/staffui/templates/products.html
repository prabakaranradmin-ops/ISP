Products & charges

What an ISP sells besides bandwidth: installation, router rental, a static IP, add-ons. A one-time charge goes on the subscriber's next invoice and is then done with; a recurring charge is billed on every renewal until you end it. Each appears as its own line on the GST tax invoice under its own HSN/SAC code, taxed in its own right. Charges are attached to a subscriber from their Billing tab.
