Served this cycle without an invoice

Active, inside a paid-up cycle on a plan that costs something, with no invoice since the cycle began (or two since the previous one began, which covers renewing early from the portal). Usual causes: a plan change, which grants the new plan's validity without charging; an expiry date edited by hand; or a renewal whose invoice failed to save.

Wallet balance that disagrees with the ledger

The stored balance should equal every wallet credit less every debit, and the balance the newest ledger entry left. A difference means the balance was changed without a ledger entry — by hand, by an import, or by a failed posting — and the customer may be owed money or owe it.

Last billed at a price other than the catalogue's

The newest plan invoice's taxable amount against the plan's price today. One-off charges are left out. Usual causes: the catalogue price changed since (the next renewal charges the new price), the plan was changed after the last invoice, or the customer paid a different amount through the portal.
