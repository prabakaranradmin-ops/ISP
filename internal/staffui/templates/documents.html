Archived documents

Durable copies of a subscriber's invoices and KYC documents, held outside the database with a SHA-256 checksum recorded from the bytes as written.

Every read is verified. A document is re-hashed when it is fetched and compared against the checksum taken when it was archived — so a copy that has been altered or damaged since is refused rather than handed over. There is deliberately no way to download one without that check: a warning next to a successful download is not a control.
