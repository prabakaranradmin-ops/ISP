Colours are hex codes such as #1a6dcc or #16c, as a brand guide gives them. The button's label is chosen automatically — white or black, whichever reads on the accent — so a pale brand colour never produces a button nobody can read. The page background is used in light mode only; a phone in dark mode keeps the dark background it asked for.
