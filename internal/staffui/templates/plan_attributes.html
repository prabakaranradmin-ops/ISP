RADIUS attributes

Extra attributes sent in a plan's Access-Accept, on top of the speed its bandwidth profile sets. Use these to put a plan's subscribers in a router address list, allocate from a pool configured on the router, or cut a trial plan's sessions after a fixed time.

Every attribute is checked against what this system knows how to send. A router silently ignores an attribute it does not understand, so a misspelled name or a wrongly typed value would leave you with a plan that looks configured and does nothing — which is why an unknown attribute is refused here rather than stored.
