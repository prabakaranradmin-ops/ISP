How long tickets actually took, as distinct from the Ticket resolution report beside it, which counts them against their SLA — a month can be entirely within SLA and still be getting slower. First arrival at resolved, not the last: a ticket closed, reopened and closed again is a support failure, and the last timestamp would report it as one slow success. Still-open counts tickets raised in the month that have never been resolved, because a fast median over a month that left thirty open is not a fast month.
