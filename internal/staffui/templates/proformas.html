Invoices Payments Proformas

A proforma is a quotation with GST estimated, numbered PF-… so it never takes a tax invoice number. Once the customer has paid, Convert charges their wallet and issues the GST tax invoice, with tax at the rate in force that day. For plan prepayments, just record the payment: each renewal raises its own tax invoice, so converting as well would charge them twice. Proformas are issued from a subscriber's Billing tab.
