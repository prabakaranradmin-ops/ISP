Plans

Editing a price re-prices every subscriber on the plan from their next renewal — the renewal reads the plan's price when it bills. Retiring a plan stops it being offered for new assignment and leaves the subscribers already on it exactly where they are, renewing at its price, because that is the tariff they bought. To re-price without touching existing customers, retire the plan and duplicate it at the new price. Download as CSV.
